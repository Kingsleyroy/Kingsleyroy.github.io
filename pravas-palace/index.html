Weddings, receptions, conferences and concerts, under one roof. Scroll down to walk through the hall, the dining hall and the rooms, just as they are.

Pravas Palace is a palace-style convention hall in Omandhur, Tindivanam, built for weddings and family functions as well as corporate meetings and events. It has a grand chandelier hall, a dining hall, luxury rooms, a lift, a Kalyana Vinayagar shrine, power backup and big car parking inside the compound.

Questions families ask

The hall team will confirm every detail when you message.

How early should we book?

For wedding dates, as early as you can, especially around auspicious days. Message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you if your date is free.

Is the hall air-conditioned?

Yes. The main hall is fully centralised AC, and the hall has a power backup unit.

How many guests can sit?

The main hall has 1000+ chairs, and the dining hall seats 500.

Is there a lift and parking?

Yes. The building has a lift, and there is big car parking inside the compound wall.

Are there rooms for the families?

Yes, the hall has luxury rooms where the families can rest and get ready.

Can we visit before booking?

Of course. Watch the walkthrough here first, then come and see the hall with your family.
