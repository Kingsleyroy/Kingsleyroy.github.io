A full-service kalyana mandapam built for South Indian weddings, receptions and family functions, with a stage made for the muhurtham, a dining hall for banana-leaf meals, rooms for both families and parking for your guests.

கேள்விகள்

Questions families ask

Sample answers. The hall will confirm the real terms.

How early should we book?

For wedding dates, as early as you can, especially around auspicious days. Message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you if your date is free.

Can we bring our own caterer?

Yes. The attached kitchen can be used by in-house or outside caterers.

Who arranges the stage decoration?

We work with trusted decorators, or you can bring your own. Share your theme and we will plan it with you.

Is the hall air-conditioned?

Yes. The main hall and the dining hall are fully air-conditioned, with generator backup.

Can we visit before booking?

Of course. Take the 3D tour here first, then come and see the hall with your family.
